\section*{What Is a Logit?}

A \textbf{logit} is the \textbf{raw score} produced by the model \emph{before} softmax converts the scores into probabilities.

For example, suppose the possible classes are:
\[
[\text{World},\ \text{Sports},\ \text{Business},\ \text{Sci/Tech}]
\]

\begin{stepbox}{Example: Raw Logits}
The model might produce:
\[
\text{logits} = [1.2,\ 0.5,\ 3.8,\ 0.9]
\]

These are \textbf{not probabilities} --- they can be negative, positive, and don't need to sum to 1.

After applying \textbf{softmax}:
\[
\text{softmax(logits)} = [0.06,\ 0.03,\ 0.87,\ 0.04]
\]

Now they \emph{are} probabilities, and they sum to 1.
\end{stepbox}

\[
\boxed{\text{Logit} = \text{raw confidence score before softmax}}
\]
